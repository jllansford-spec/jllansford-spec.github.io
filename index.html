VALORANT

Design lead, Maps team and Modes team

Lotus

Map

VALORANT's second three-site map, built around rotating doors that open new routes mid-round. With Lotus, we made three-site maps a core VALORANT design. The map also has some unique natural geo that creates novel gunplay.

Lotus on the VALORANT wiki

Pearl

Map

A three-lane map with a compact mid and no additional mechanics. After exploring several new styles of map over the last few releases, we went back to our roots with this one. While there are new ideas within, the core of the map is straightforward and comfortable.

Pearl on the VALORANT wiki

Fracture

Map

A unique map where Defenders get pinched by Attackers from both sides. The map poses the question, “who is neutral space really for?” Players must think differently about how they defend and hold sites.

Fracture on the VALORANT wiki

Range

Map

In addition to building several of the training systems, I also built a jump course. A throwback to CS jump maps, it was designed as a lighthearted introduction to VALORANT's movement mechanics, with room to explore that other maps don't allow.

Range on the VALORANT wiki

Modes and champions

Systems updates on Howling Abyss, Twisted Treeline and Dominion, plus champion balance and reworks.

Howling Abyss

Patch 6.12

Overhauled several systems to shorten game length and reduce locked game states. Average game time dropped by 2 minutes without increasing number of blowouts.

Patch 6.12

Twisted Treeline

Patch 5.11

Made several updates, but the largest one added a tower in every lane to improve pacing, new items, reworked altars and a Vilemaw encounter with a new buff to increase terminal game outcomes.

Patch 5.11

Dominion

Soul Anchor

I created the revive trinket, Soul Anchor. Removing the need for the Revive summoner spell made many more champions viable in the mode.

Soul Anchor

Champions

Patches 5.20 to 7.4

  • Cho'Gath: Delivered the infinite stacking fantasy while serving both the Tank and AP builds. His play rate rose greatly.
  • Anivia: Reworked her ultimate to push her power toward the late game and sharpen her zone control.
  • Urgot: Added a fear bomb to his ultimate for better engage and survivability, along with several quality of life changes.
  • Dr. Mundo: Turned his E into an attack reset. The health cost is only paid when the attack lands, using his crit headbutt animation (was previously unused as Mundo doesn't build crit).
